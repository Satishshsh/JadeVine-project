About Our School

Founded in 1995, Asteria Academy of Excellence offers quality education from Nursery to Class 12. We believe every child is unique and deserves a joyful learning journey. Our vision is to nurture responsible, confident and compassionate learners. Our mission is to combine quality education with creativity, discipline and respect. Students are encouraged to ask questions, work together and discover their strengths.

OUR MOTTO: "Education is the key that opens the door to opportunity."
